# Disaggregation

## Overview

Disaggregation separates resources that are conventionally packaged together so they can be pooled or scaled independently. Compute, memory, storage, or other services may remain physically distinct while still participating in one logical workload through a high-bandwidth interconnect or service layer.

## Why It Matters

AI systems often have imbalanced resource demands. Disaggregation can improve utilization or capacity scaling, but it makes data movement, latency, bandwidth, locality, and failure behavior more visible because access crosses a physical boundary that would otherwise be local.
