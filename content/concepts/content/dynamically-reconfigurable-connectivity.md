# Dynamically reconfigurable connectivity

## Overview

Dynamically reconfigurable connectivity allows the active physical or circuit-level path between endpoints to change under control software. Optical circuit switches are one example: rather than forwarding every packet electronically, the system can alter which optical paths are connected.

## Why It Matters

AI workloads can change their communication pattern across jobs or phases. Reconfigurable connectivity can adapt the fabric to those patterns without changing endpoint identity or canonical containment, but it introduces control, scheduling, and reconfiguration constraints that static topologies do not have.
