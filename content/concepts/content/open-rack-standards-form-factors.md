# Open rack standards and form factors

## Overview

Open rack standards define physical conventions for racks, shelves, power delivery, serviceability, and compatible hardware modules. They shape how servers, storage devices, network equipment, power shelves, and cooling components are arranged and maintained as a coherent physical system.

## Why It Matters

Form factor is not merely cosmetic in AI infrastructure. It determines enclosure boundaries, rack density, power and cooling integration, cable routing, and what constitutes a replaceable assembly, so it can materially change the hierarchy that Explore should present.
