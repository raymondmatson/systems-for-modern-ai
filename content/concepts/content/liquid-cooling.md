# Liquid cooling

## Overview

Liquid cooling moves heat from high-power compute components through coolant rather than relying only on room air. In direct-to-chip systems, cold plates and coolant loops sit close to processors or accelerators, while pumps, distribution units, and heat exchangers move the captured heat toward facility infrastructure.

## Why It Matters

Rack-scale AI systems can reach power densities that make conventional air cooling difficult or inefficient. Cooling therefore becomes part of the physical architecture: it affects rack design, serviceability, power headroom, thermal throttling risk, and where support equipment must be placed.
